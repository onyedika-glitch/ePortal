School Announcements

FEATURED - October 11, 2025

Mid-Term Break and Resumption Dates

This is to inform all students, parents, and guardians that the school will observe its mid-term break from Monday, October 27th to Friday, October 31st, 2025. All academic activities will resume on Monday, November 3rd, 2025. We wish everyone a restful break.

October 10, 2025

2025-2026 School Calendar Approved

The board has officially approved the academic calendar for the upcoming school year. Key dates include resumption on September 15th and first term exams starting December 1st. You can download the full calendar from the resources section.

October 5, 2025

New Digital Literacy Initiative Launched

We are excited to announce a new program to provide tablets and coding classes to all junior secondary students, starting this term. A detailed schedule for device distribution will be sent to parents via email.

September 30, 2025

Inter-House Sports Heats Schedule

The preliminary heats for the annual inter-house sports competition will take place from November 17th to 20th. All participating students should confirm their events with their house masters.
